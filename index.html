What is moving this score

Compared with a typical account. Red pushes risk up, green pulls it down.

Logistic regression from our MIST 5400 notebook, trained on 2,400 synthetic commercial accounts (ROC-AUC 0.77 on 600 held-out accounts). It scores the next renewal, not long-term loyalty. Use it to decide who gets attention first, and check the context with the client team before acting.
